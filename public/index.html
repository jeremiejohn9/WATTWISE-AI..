About WATTWISE

⚡ Smart Classroom Energy Management

WATTWISE is a smart classroom model designed to reduce unnecessary electricity use. PIR sensors detect movement, an LDR measures light intensity, and the system controls eight classroom LEDs based on the conditions.

The DHT11 sensor monitors temperature and humidity. The dashboard displays sensor readings, lighting status and estimated energy use.

Energy calculations are estimates based on assumed LED power. Actual energy savings require real power measurements.
